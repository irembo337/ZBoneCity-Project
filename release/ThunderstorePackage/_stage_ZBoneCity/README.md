# ZBoneCity

For questions write me on Discord: `naxock`

Discord server: https://discord.gg/fAwJYjCPSq

ZBoneCity is a BETA tactical medical overhaul for BONELAB.

## Features

- Full organ system: heart, brain, lungs, and more
- Realistic pain and unconsciousness
- Bleeding with blood pools and blood trails
- Fractures and limb damage
- Fullscreen pain and blackout effects
- Knife penetration system
- Advanced medical system: morphine, bandages, tourniquets, splints, blood packs, and more
- NPC trauma reactions
- Medical HUD and Medical Menu
- BoneMenu integration
- Integrated AXON-style Bodycam
- Custom impact, headshot, death, and neck snap sounds
- VR optimized systems

## Neck Snap Sound

The neck snap mechanic uses `snapcracklepop.wav` from:

`UserData/ZBoneCity/Audio/snapcracklepop.wav`

## Requirements

- BONELAB
- MelonLoader
- BoneLib / BoneMenu
